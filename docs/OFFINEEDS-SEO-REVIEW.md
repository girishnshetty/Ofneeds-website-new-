Reviewed on 3 October 2026. Sources are the live public [homepage](https://www.offineeds.com/), [KitUp onboarding page](https://www.offineeds.com/kitup) and [locations page](https://www.offineeds.com/location). This is an on-page review, not a ranking, traffic, backlink or paid-ad report. No OfNeeds title, description, keyword strategy, canonical, robots or structured-data changes were made for this review.

## SEO techniques actually observed

1. **Intent-specific titles and descriptions.** The homepage title positions the business as an India-wide corporate-gifting company. KitUp's title and description target onboarding and welcome kits; the locations page targets geographic delivery coverage.
2. **Dedicated service pages and contextual links.** Onboarding, branded stores, rewards and employee-choice services have separate landing pages linked from the homepage. Product-category links connect service explanations to the catalogue.
3. **Broad topical coverage.** Homepage content groups products and gifting occasions into themes and provides longer explanations of services. This is evidence of topic coverage; its ranking impact cannot be inferred from the page alone.
4. **Buyer-question content.** The homepage and onboarding page answer questions about quantities, personalization, samples, kit configuration and delivery. These match practical procurement questions.
5. **Technical homepage metadata.** The live DOM contains a self-referencing HTTPS canonical, `index, follow`, Open Graph title, and JSON-LD blocks with `Organization`, `WebSite` and `FAQPage` types. This audit confirms their presence, not eligibility for rich results or validity of every field.
6. **Local service coverage.** A dedicated locations page describes their delivery network. The site also presents contact and business information. Do not infer that OfNeeds has the same offices, logistics, certifications or partnerships.

The homepage H1 focuses on a branding platform rather than repeating its gifting-company title. The reviewed onboarding and locations pages did not expose a meta-keywords tag in the live DOM. A browser can inspect rendered metadata; search snapshots may show only page text.
